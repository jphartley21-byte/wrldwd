For Joe's ear. Everything below fits the original's audio frame (toolkit/fitbar.py) and its by-ear rhyme scheme; the difference is
what the bars DO. Left: v5, written from the stories in time order. Right: the new order (premise -> section argument -> moves ->
ingredients -> sentences -> fit). Numbers are from the repo's own tools; none of them can tell you which is funnier.

How to read the recount numbers: toolkit/recount.py counts bars that report a past event with no attitude, or list places in time
order. The original's par: 0 such bars in any hook section, at most 3 in a row in a verse, 20% of the song.
