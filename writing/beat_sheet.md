# Beat sheet - one plain sentence per bar, roles copied from the original's jobs

Written 11 Oct 2026 after the v18 review. Rule 2: no words until this is agreed.

## The spine (one premise for the whole track)
**"Never in one place."** A bloke who has spent fourteen years moving (Joe's real 2012-2026), reporting it flat. His whole life fits in a rucksack and on a black box of photographs. Nothing illicit happens; the menace is the tone and the borders. It adds up to nothing he can show anyone, and he says so without complaining.

Why this spine and not the original's: the original's engine is dealing (a global distribution round). Ours cannot be - the truth is travel, so the engine is **logistics applied to a life**: ferries, receipts, checks, gates, hostels, fines, the drive. Same technique (bigness reported in a clerk's register), different world.

## Hook 5-8  [BUILT + VERIFIED 11 Oct: meaning gate PASS, BEATS the slot on 4 criteria, nothing below par]
5. A bare hello in the next country's language, then the long road he came in on. [t: Ola (Portuguese), Porto 2023 motorbike]
6. He is stopped again at the border and it is routine. [t/p: the gendarme fine, Pyrenees 2025]
7. He is let go with a glass of water - the state's whole apparatus ends in tap water. [p]
8. He might miss the boat in the morning, said as a fact. [p] - idiom cashed literally, modal understatement
End family: AO + a weak syllable (Porto / border / water / morning) - the original's H3 shape, soft 4/4.
Register flip: border (the state's line) -> water (nothing). Perfect rhyme pair, the word change IS the payoff.
