## The conflict that is left, stated plainly

Bar 40's frame asks for a three-syllable tail ending STRONG (`|SwS`) -- Castletown is exactly that -- while
bars 37 and 39 ask for a tail that is one WEAK syllable (`|w`), which only a weak function word gives
("him", "in"). `Castletown ~ him` measures 0.00: a strong-vowelled tail cannot rhyme with a weak one. The
original escapes this because its own last bar ends on a weak-ish nasal ("-zon" ~ "-him" = 0.80). So either
the true place moves INSIDE a bar and the block ends on a weak-tail place, or bar 40 keeps Castletown and
bars 37-39 have to end on the -own family. Joe's call, and cheap to test either way.
