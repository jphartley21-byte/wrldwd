## Gaps the judges name, consistent across all seven sections (abstract)
- **Persona**: candidates report errands in a mild voice; the original's narrator carries swagger and stakes in the sound. Cast (Keith, Dave) is handed the action and the narrator becomes an onlooker.
- **Rhyme carpet**: the original holds one vowel family across the whole 8-bar block with dense internal and multisyllabic rhyme; candidates rhyme a few bars then drift into unrhymed reportage (V1 25-32 12/28, V2 9-16 13/28, V1 33-44 39/66 pairs).
- **Payoff in the rhyme slot**: stock phrases are cashed, but mid-bar rather than as the last word; end words are often suffix-rhymes (-ings, -ayer) not double-reading words.
- **Flow**: bar-length variation is accidental; several bars run 14-16 syllables and are not speakable at pace.
- **12-bar V1 33-44 is the weakest**: too many unrelated beats; the original runs one story with a bribe, an attitude switch and a debt.
