# Story seeds v4 (13 Oct 2026): what the writer is told for each section

Assistant-authored from material/travel_history.md and place_specifics.md; NOT approved by Joe. Every event is real; exaggeration is marked (cartoon).

## The premise
A flat-voiced Bristol man (originally Reading) in his thirties who has been to every party and every adventure he could get to, and reports them the way other people report errands: a lime wristband, a ferry ramp, a hostel booking, a rail pass, a haggle, a stag weekend. He is the actor of every line (I went, I paid, I stood there), the others are roles (the lads, the crowd, the deckhand, the stall man, the barman) and each boast is deflated by one small concrete detail from the trip. EVERY event in the song is something Joe really did (material/travel_history.md and material/place_specifics.md): Boomtown, Rolling Loud Rotterdam, King's Day in Amsterdam, the 2019 Interrail, the 2015-17 backpacking, four motorbike tours, Marrakesh, the Naples stag weekend. Exaggeration is allowed only as cartoon deadpan on a true event and is flagged '(cartoon)'. The joke engine is wordplay in the rhyme slot (a stock phrase cashed into a literal object, a pun pair), not plot.
