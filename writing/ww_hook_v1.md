## Where it still falls short (the judges, abstractly, consistent across rounds)
- **Flow** is the biggest gap (new blocks about 6 against the original's 9): bar lengths too even, little strong/weak variation, internal rhyme thinner than the target.
- **Character**: a plausible operator voice, but the swagger is stated rather than carried by the sound.
- **Register flip**: the cross-world rhyme pair is only partly achieved; most pairs are same-sound nouns with one real second sense (not cricket / eleven on one, off the back of a lorry).
- Hook 9-16 is much further back: the long roll-call bar and the two interleaved rhyme groups are still the hardest structure to satisfy together with sense.
