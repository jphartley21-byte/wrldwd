# Five-question card (replaces rubric + gates G1-G6 as the default; Joe's ear decides)
1. Would Joe say it to a mate, word for word?  (voice/profile.md, voice/calibration.md)
2. Does the bar say something (a thing, a place, a joke, a flex)?
3. Does it feel good out loud: rhythm, rhyme, one breath?  (toolkit output is a hint, not a verdict)
4. True or flagged: from material/ or Joe, else mark (invented) or (persona).
5. Distinct from the original: no copied frames or phrases; the original is guidance for shape and flow only.
Voice beats craft when they conflict. Log the craft note and move on.
