# Joe's "Worldwide" track: a Suno rap in the style of Pete & Bas "Mr Worldwide"

@STATE.md

## What this is
Joe (33, from Reading, lives in Bristol) wants a Suno track that sounds like he wrote it: a deadpan British world-tour song at 140 BPM built from his REAL travel history, as good as the original in craft. Audience: Joe and his mates. Swearing and vulgar slang are allowed and never discouraged (Joe, 13 Oct 2026), as are drug, gun, weapon and money slang and pop-culture references; deadpan drug and violence imagery is allowed (as in the original) but nothing instructional. Joe expects a long iterative process and wants calibrated, honest feedback: never call bars good without hedging. He is cost-sensitive and has hit usage limits: keep replies short, no waste.

## Rules (hard-won in the first sessions)
1. COMEDY = CHARACTER + WORDPLAY, sold by rhyme scheme and flow. Joe: "there's not really any jokes at all". Character: something big or illicit reported flatly in an everyday British register (parcel delivery, pub, telling-off, prices), domestic person MENTIONED not addressed. Wordplay: a stock phrase cashed into a literal thing, the payoff word in the rhyme slot (last word). No setup-punchline gags, no puns that announce themselves. Evidence: benchmark/comedy_theory.md, benchmark/genius_line_classes.md.
2. EVERY BAR MUST SAY SOMETHING. Agree a one-line-per-bar beat sheet with Joe BEFORE writing words. Round-1 and round-2 bars "said nothing" and were rejected.
3. SOUND MAP FIRST, THEN WORDS. Choose a vowel carpet, a consonant skeleton and at least one perfect-rhyme pair from the toolkit; build bar by bar; run `check` on every bar; end words come from toolkit banks. One-syllable AAA triples (puff/huff/stuff) are too weak. The original's chorus bars are 5-10 syllables (mean 8); its verse lines go up to 15.
4. NEVER reproduce the original's lyrics in files or replies beyond isolated words; cite by line number (Ch L1, V1 L12). Joe can re-paste them in chat when needed.
5. TRUE OR FLAGGED: facts come from material/. Anything else is "(invented)" or "(persona)" for Joe to confirm. No hobby or nerd material. Real place, people, film, TV and music names ARE available (Joe, 11 Oct 2026: "the pool should also include things like place names, peoples names, movies, tv shows, music related") - use them for texture and specificity, not as the joke, and mind that a name cannot be invented (it is either real or flagged). SWEARING AND VULGAR SLANG ARE ALLOWED AND NEVER DISCOURAGED (Joe, 13 Oct 2026: "all swear words and vulgar slang should be available to use and never discouraged"), as are the slang of drugs, guns, weapons and money, and pop-culture references (films, TV, music, games, brands, memes, famous people). No instructional drug detail. Do not copy the original's frames ("[greeting] to the boys in [place]", the plane/plain pun, "tell her ... back shortly").
6. CHARACTERS, CAPPED (Joe, 13 Oct 2026): "limit any reference to a character to 2 references. prefer romantic partners or mates over family." So at most TWO references to any one person in the whole song (the lads, my mate, the copper, the debtor, Mum), and the personal detail comes from a mate or a partner rather than a parent. Enforced by `toolkit/characters.py` (cap 2), the plan gate per block, and the per-bar ratchet song-wide; measured on the two songs: the original has 0 family references (2 partners, 7 mates) and our v7 had 11 family references and no partner at all. The cap is stricter than the benchmark (the original's largest count is "mate" x4) and is therefore a rule, not a calibrated gate.
6. COST. Subagents inherit this session's max reasoning effort and ran away: 6 of 8 writer agents burned 128,000 output tokens each and returned nothing. Do NOT launch writer or judge agents unless Joe asks and the effort is lowered. Work in the main thread with the toolkit. Do not re-read big files or redo research that is already in benchmark/.
7. SUNO: v6 only. Joe signs in himself. Fill title, lyrics, style and exclude fields, screenshot, then STOP before Create until Joe says yes. Variety 0, Style Influence about 70, Weirdness about 30, vocal gender male; exclude: singing, melodic hook, autotune, choir, female vocals. Test 140 BPM and 70 half-time (the original measures exactly 140.00 BPM, 13 Oct 2026). Details in brief/suno_rap_rules.md. Leave the key out of the prompt. Artist names are fine in the LYRICS (Joe, 14 Oct 2026); keep them out of the style, exclude and title fields.
8. DECISIONS STILL OPEN (ask Joe once, concisely): strict vegetarian detail in the song? Horfield/BS7 as a name? everyday British brand names (the original uses them)? invented cartoonish flourishes on true places (a bribe, a rival firm)?

## Commands
`/write <sections>` drafts with parallel agents until every check passes; `/review <draft or slot>` runs every check (toolkit/check_all.py + human gates). Original lyrics stay in C:SERSJOEDOCUMENTSWORLDWIDE_PRIVATE (NEVER IN THE PROJECT).
ML LAYER (the 17 HUMAN gates, no agents): `python ml/judge.py FILE|-` prints per-bar rejection risk + the drivers behind it; `python ml/evaluate.py` must PASS before it is trusted; retrain after adding verdicts with `python ml/build_dataset.py` then `python ml/train_judge.py`. It ranks and explains - a low risk is not "good".
LOCAL GENERATOR (uses Joe's GPU, no per-token cost): `python ml/generate.py --slot "Hook 5-8" -n 20` generates candidates from a local Ollama model and keeps only those passing the meaning gate + the judge; survivors land in writing/generated_<slot>.md and every candidate is logged to ml/data/generated.jsonl. `check_all` is reported as a RANKING signal, not a veto: it measures similarity to the original's audio and Joe's approved bars 1-4 fail it.
RESEARCH + VOCAB (the offline/online lookups, no agents): `python toolkit/research.py word|rhymes|near|sounds|mine|slang WORD/QUERY` (Datamuse + Wiktionary online, `mine` local); every lookup is banked in `toolkit/vocab_ledger.jsonl` (see `python toolkit/vocab.py show`), online results are cached so a repeat query is free, and each word carries a G2 recognisability PROXY. G2 never vetoes a word - it is US-weighted and flags real British slang; Joe's ear decides. Only a headword (<= 3 words) ever leaves the machine, never a lyric line or material/.
FORGE (exhaustive search, no agents, no LLM, no input needed until the end): `python toolkit/forge.py --slot "Hook 5-8"` mines every bar and block already in writing/, enumerates every combination matching the slot's measured scheme, filters on the meaning gate, and ranks by an objective weighted on the human-gate judge. Output: writing/forge_<slot>.md. `--selftest` must rediscover Joe's locked bars.
POP-CULTURE LORE (ww/lex/cards.py + graph.py, 13 Oct 2026): `python -m ww.cli kit SLOT` prints the section's kit (names ranked by sound + scene connections, no model calls); `ww probe --pilot` counts connections by tier; `ww card NAME --build` compiles one lore card (~0.02 cent); `ww argue SLOT --kit` gives the seed stage the kit (cards for its best candidates are compiled lazily, ~0.4 cent) and the `lore_allusion` mechanism. Cards are unverified model associations (rule 5): flag, never assert. See STATE.md 13 Oct.
PIPELINE (continuous, no agents, no LLM): `python toolkit/pipeline.py` loops the forge over all 9 open slots forever, pools only NEW quads that clear the judge bar (risk < 0.45, so the corpus cannot rot), and writes writing/pipeline_{log.md,results.jsonl,status.json,pool.md}. `--once` / `--cycles N` bound it. STOP IT by creating writing/PIPELINE_STOP.

MOUTH + DURATION (15 Oct 2026, report-only): `python toolkit/duration.py --slot-bar N -` (syllable time needed vs the template's step gap at 140/70), `flow_check.vowel_travel()`, reference benchmark/mouth_and_duration.md. Not yet calibrated on the original.

## Stuck rule (Joe, 9 Oct 2026)
Count every version of a bar set shown to Joe in STATE.md ("Attempts"). PIVOT when any trigger fires:
- T1: 3 versions in a row rejected or "not landing" for the same bars.
- T2: the same end-sound family or theme used in 3 rejected versions (the constraint is the problem, not the words).
- T3: a fix for one gate breaks another twice (e.g. fixing flow kills meaning).
- T4: the end-sound bank has fewer than 6 words that pass gate G2 (recognisable), checked with `rhymes`.
PIVOT protocol: park the best version in writing/contenders.md with one line on why it stalled; write 3 fresh angles (each: one-line beat sheet, end-sound family, a 6+ word G2 bank, true places) that change at least two of theme / rhyme family / structure; pick one by gates G1-G3; build it from scratch without reusing the stalled lines. Tell Joe in one line that a pivot fired and why. Joe's own lines are inputs, not fixed constraints (gate G4).

## Definition of done for a section (Joe, 9 Oct 2026; FORM redefined 11 Oct 2026)
`toolkit/meaning_gate.py` PASS AND gates G1-G6 + job check PASS AND `toolkit/ratchet.py` against the previous draft says OK (no regressions, no keepers in toolkit/keepers.json lost, and no RISE in form problems). Agents keep looping until then (hard cap 10 passes for cost), applying the stuck rule to any bar that fails 3 passes.
`toolkit/check_all.py` is a RANKING TARGET, not a veto (Joe's decision, 11 Oct 2026). It measures SIMILARITY to the original's audio-measured rhythm, and Joe's own APPROVED bars 1-4 fail it (bar 2 is 8 syllables vs the original's 9; the stress grid and end shape miss). So: get the count with `python toolkit/check_all.py --score --slot "NAME" -`, drive it DOWN against the previous draft, and use it to rank generator candidates (ml/generate.py). Exact form is the aim; it is not the definition of done.
