# Place assumptions: what a traveller typically does and the stories that go with it (14 Oct 2026)

Joe's instruction: assume he did the typical things in the places he went, and embellish them. EVERYTHING here is ASSUMED or generic
traveller lore, written from general knowledge, NOT verified against Joe's photos or emails and NOT researched online. Rule 5: it is
flagged "(assumed)" in the song's notes, never asserted as a fact about Joe. Confirmed by Joe in chat: weed in Amsterdam, the red light
district, partying in Sydney, the beach. Places and years come from material/travel_history.md.

## Amsterdam (2013, 2014, 2017, 2019 Interrail and King's Day, 2023)
Typical: a coffee shop menu read like a wine list; weed on a canal bench; walking through the red light district eyes front; hire bikes and
nearly get run down by a local with a bell; canal boat; stroopwafel; Heineken Experience; chips with mayo; the last tram missed; orange
everything on King's Day (flea market on every pavement, boats jammed on the canals). Story shapes: lost the hostel, found it by smell;
the shortcut that turns out to be the red light district.
## Rotterdam (2023, Rolling Loud)
Typical: festival wristband, tram out, Markthal, cube houses. Material fact: the giant garden gnome. Embellish: the gnome comes home.
## Sydney (2015, 2017)
Typical: Bondi to Coogee coastal walk, the Opera House photo everyone takes, the Manly ferry, Kings Cross pub crawl, a flat white,
sunburn in the shape of a flip-flop, seagulls stealing chips, the Harbour Bridge. Story shapes: partied in the Cross, slept on Bondi sand.
## Melbourne (2017 St Kilda)
Typical: laneway coffee, the tram, St Kilda penguins at dusk, the Great Ocean Road day trip, Aussie rules at the MCG.
## Byron Bay, Noosa, Brisbane, Port Macquarie
Byron: surf lesson and drowned dignity, lighthouse sunrise, campervans, hippies. Noosa: national park koala hunt, surf. Brisbane: South
Bank lagoon, Story Bridge. Port Macquarie: koala hospital, the beach.
## K'gari / Fraser Island and Rainbow Beach (2017)
Typical: hire a 4WD, drive the beach at low tide, a dingo warning sign, the Maheno shipwreck, Lake McKenzie, getting stuck in sand, the tide
as the timetable. Story shapes: got the 4WD stuck; a dingo ate a mate's sandwich.
## Hervey Bay, Whitsundays, Magnetic Island, Townsville, Cairns
Hervey Bay: whale watching. Whitsundays: a sailing trip, Whitehaven Beach's white silica sand, a stinger suit. Magnetic Island: koalas, a hire moke.
Cairns: Great Barrier Reef snorkel, crocodile warning signs, the Kuranda railway, a backpacker bar.
## Backpacking 2015-16: Laos, Vietnam, Bangkok, Koh Tao, Christchurch, Perth, Bali, Gili Trawangan
Laos: river tubing; Vietnam: scooters, pho on a plastic stool, Ha Long Bay; Bangkok: Khao San Road, a tuk-tuk with a "special price" detour; Koh Tao: diving,
buckets of spirits, Full Moon. Christchurch: the rebuilt city, a campervan, bungee. Bali: Kuta, Bintang, monkeys stealing sunglasses, Bali belly.
Gili Trawangan: no cars, horse carts, the beach swing at sunset, the cheap hostel party.
## Interrail 2019: Zurich, Bern, Interlaken/Lauterbrunnen, Florence, Ljubljana, Bratislava, Vienna, Zell am See, Munich, Augsburg, Black Forest
Zurich: expensive (a pint costs a rail pass). Bern: bears. Lauterbrunnen: Staubbach Falls, cowbells. Florence: David, gelato queue, 463 steps up the
Duomo, haggling for a leather jacket. Ljubljana: the dragons on the bridge, the castle funicular. Bratislava: the UFO bridge, the man-at-work statue.
Vienna: a schnitzel bigger than the plate, Freud, Sachertorte. Zell am See: the lake. Munich: a litre mug, pretzels, the Hofbrauhaus, Bayern.
Black Forest: cuckoo clocks, cherry cake, the honesty-box drinks stall. Story shapes: slept through a station and woke in the wrong country.
