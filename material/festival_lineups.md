## Rolling Loud Rotterdam, Ahoy, 30 Jun - 1 Jul 2023 (MOJO / Woo Hah!; four stages: a big outdoor Mainstage, two indoor stages, an outdoor talent stage)
- Headliners on the bill: Travis Scott and Kendrick Lamar. Joe's own list: Kendrick Lamar, Central Cee, JID.
- Also on the bill: Metro Boomin, GloRilla, Soulja Boy, Latto, Lil Uzi Vert, Denzel Curry, Yung Lean, Ken Carson, Ski Mask The Slump God, Lakeyah; UK: Central Cee, Aitch, D-Block Europe; Afrobeats: Rema, Ayra Starr; Dutch: SFB, Idaly, Lijpe, Bokoesam, Cho, Brunzyn.
- Photo stamps (real): lime-green and pale-blue wristbands behind metal barriers, a blue-arched stage lit teal and purple, a raised metal walkway, an orange carton drink; the giant bronze gnome in a brick square the same weekend.

## Boomtown, Matterley Estate near Winchester (every August; Joe: "all the drugs, dancing")
- 2019, Chapter 11 "A Radical City", 8-11 Aug (wet; tents collapsed): headliners Ms Lauryn Hill, The Streets, Prophets of Rage; also Chronixx, UB40 ft. Ali Campbell and Astro, Groove Armada, Salt-N-Pepa (Town Centre), Atari Teenage Riot; later additions Noisia, Flogging Molly.
- 2022, "Chapter One: The Gathering", 10-14 Aug (dusty; the first full Boomtown since 2019): no single headliner (the lineup was released days before, only in an app); named acts Four Tet, De La Soul, Fabio and Grooverider with the Outlook Orchestra, Squarepusher, Koffee, Moonchild Sanelly, Nia Archives, Dan Shake, Nastia, Mall Grab b2b My Nu Leng, Shy FX, DJ Marky, and Noisia's last-ever UK show (Origin stage, 11 pm Saturday).
- 2023, "Chapter Two: The Twin Trail", 9-13 Aug (15th anniversary): The Prodigy and Cypress Hill; Loyle Carner, Peaches, Sister Sledge, Andy C, Mura Masa, Nia Archives, Sudan Archives, Confidence Man, Dimension, Eats Everything, Helena Hauff.
- 2024, "Chapter 3: Revolution of Imagination", 7-11 Aug (photos: a wooden guillotine prop, WANTED FOR EXCESSIVE ENERGY posters, a free-tattoo tent): billed The Pharcyde, Mark Rebillet, Ibibio Sound Machine, Al McKay's Earth Wind and Fire Experience; Fri Ezra Collective, Sleaford Mods, Caravan Palace, Dub Pistols; Sat Greentea Peng, Gentlemans Dub Club, Soft Play, Flogging Molly; Sun Damian Marley, Fat Freddy's Drop, Henge, a Pendulum DJ set, Beans on Toast at 3 pm at the Engine House; also Viagra Boys, Skream and Benga, SBTRKT, Ghetts, Asian Dub Foundation (30th anniversary), Bob Vylan.
- 2025, 6-10 Aug (bone dry): Sex Pistols featuring Frank Carter, Sean Paul, Maribou State; Overmono, Nia Archives, Kojey Radical, Koffee, Azealia Banks, Honey Dijon, Rudimental, Souls of Mischief, Goldie (live), Miss Dynamite, Joy Orbison, Pa Salieu, Girls Don't Sync, Craig Charles, Babylon Circus; a Radio 1 Dance takeover of the Lion's Gate stage on the Friday.
- Facts that repeat: four to five days, over 450 artists, 12 districts, 15 stages and 50 hidden venues, the Lion's Den and Grand Central stages, the Town Centre district.

## Reading Festival (with Leeds), Reading
- 2013, 23-25 Aug: Main Stage headliners Eminem, Biffy Clyro, Green Day; also Nine Inch Nails, System of a Down, Bring Me the Horizon, Fall Out Boy, Alt-J, Foals, Jake Bugg, Deftones, Sub Focus, Boy Better Know.
- 2017, 25-27 Aug (Joe's photos 26-27 Aug): Friday Kasabian; Saturday Eminem, with Major Lazer, Giggs, Korn (their Reading debut); Sunday Muse; also Two Door Cinema Club, Bastille, Haim, Glass Animals, Blossoms, Liam Gallagher.

## Wasteland Summerfest, Amsterdam, Sat 1 Jul 2023 (post-apocalyptic fetish festival, running since 1994; Joe: "probably called Wasteland")
- No confirmed 2023 lineup was found. The festival's own DJ roster includes Joey Daniel, Annie O, Funkerman, Clark Kent, Lucien Foort and entertainment acts Mystic Tribes and Svetlana, but it is not dated to 2023. Format: live acts, fire shows, several stages.
