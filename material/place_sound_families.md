# Place-name sound families (British pronunciation) from Joe's travel history

Method (from the benchmark): choose the rhyme chain first, then pick places that fit it. Each family lists the places that end on that sound, the common words that rhyme, and any real double meanings. "Strong" = four or more places. Greetings and local words must be accurate: reactors from the named places checked the original's.

## Strong families (4+ places)
| Sound | Places (trip) | Common rhymes | Double meanings / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **-ay** (bay, day) | Byron Bay, Hervey Bay (Au 2017); Zell am See, said "zell am zay" (Interrail 2019); Pompeii, "pom-PAY" (2026); Calais (2023 tour); Torquay-type seaside; Bombay | say, play, way, okay, delay, hooray, stay, spray, display, away, today | "Byron Bay / okay / delay" (ferry and train delays); Calais = "CAL-ay".
