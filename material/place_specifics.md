### Songwriting notes on those details (not yet used)
- **72 waterfalls** is a ready-made number ladder or counting joke (one thin waterfall in the photos, seventy-two in the guidebook).
- "**aisle**" is a homophone of "isle" and "I'll" (Isle of Man is on the motorbike list) and a cut-up rhyme with "Endgame"-style words; "passed out in the aisle" is a deadpan understatement ready for a short punchline line.
- Freud museum, David and the Galileo museum are a culture-vulture bathos set against the Amsterdam and Boomtown material: a "museum run" lexical set against a "chemist's run" set.
- "Cee" (Central Cee) rhymes with "see", "Zell am See" and "sea"; artist and film names may be moderated in Suno lyrics, so use initials, nicknames or descriptions (a "Compton headliner", "the Avengers finale") rather than names.
- The Three Peaks (Ben Nevis, Scafell Pike, Snowdon) is a natural three-step ladder; Snowdon was 11 May 2024 and Scafell Pike 27 Aug 2023 in the records.
