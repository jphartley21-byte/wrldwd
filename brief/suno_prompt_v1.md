# Suno prompt v1 (9 Oct 2026). Model v6. No artist, song or brand names anywhere (moderation).
Sources: brief/suno_rap_rules.md (how to prompt), benchmark/genius_and_reviews_findings.md + reaction_findings.md (the original's sound).

## What the original sounds like (facts used)
140 BPM by songbpm (Joe tapped 142), half-time feel 70, 4/4, about 3:01, roughly one lyric line per bar. Tight UK drill production built on one catchy, instantly recognisable replayed dance-pop riff, party-ready. Raspy, explosive and smooth deep voices, deadpan delivery of excess, strong breath control, uneven line lengths on purpose (short, short, short, then long fast runs). Key left out on purpose.

## Style box (main, about 300 chars)
UK drill, party drill, gruff raspy male rap vocal, flat deadpan delivery, southern English accent, clear diction, 142 BPM half-time, sliding 808 bass, triplet hi-hat rolls, syncopated kicks, catchy looping euro-dance synth riff, bouncy, dry upfront vocal

## Variants (change ONE thing per round, two Creates each)
- B tempo: swap "142 BPM half-time" for "71 BPM".
- C two voices: swap "gruff raspy male rap vocal" for "two male rappers trading lines, one raspy and explosive, one deep and smooth".
- D colder: drop "party drill" and "bouncy", add "sparse minor-key bells".

## Exclude box
singing, melodic hook, autotune, choir, female vocals

## Controls
Vocal gender: Male. Variety 0. Style Influence 70. Weirdness 30. Max Mode only for finals.

## Title
Worldwide

## Lyrics box format
[Hook: rapped, gruff, flat deadpan]
(chorus 16 lines)
[Verse 1: rapped, flat deadpan, clear diction]
...split the 44-line verse into 4 tagged parts of about 11 lines ([Verse 1: ...], [Verse 1: faster, double-time] etc.): Suno rushes past ~12 lines per section.
[Verse 2: rapped, building, menacing deadpan]
[Hook: rapped, gruff, flat deadpan]
Rules: one line per bar; no hyphen stretches; commas only where a pause is wanted; 1-2 ad-libs in parentheses per section max.
Caution: the full song is about 96 lines; Suno skips or rushes past ~60 lines / 3,000 chars. Plan: first Create = hook + verse 1 parts 1-2; then Extend for the rest, or trim.
