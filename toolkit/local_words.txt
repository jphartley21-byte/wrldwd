# Why this file exists (12 Oct 2026). The Wiktionary harvest (loanwords.txt) filled itself from country
# translation tables, so it holds obvious primary words and MISSES the ones the original actually uses: the
# words that appear in the middle of a transaction. One of the original's best bars turns on a word an
# Italian says across a shop counter, and another on a foreign word for the police. Neither was in the
# supply, which made the code-switching engine look unused in the verses when in fact it is used five times.
#
