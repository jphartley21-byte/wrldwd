# Shared glossary for critic pools (apply equally to ALL stanzas). One entry per line: term = meaning. Joe's corrections win.
certi = UK slang, short for 'certified': authentic, cool
ouzo = Greek/Cypriot anise aperitif; 'back' = drink
needs must when the devil drives = stock phrase (you do what you have to)
twenty deck = pack of 20 cigarettes; dome = head; doobies = joints (sounds like Dubai)
plod = police; slap on the wrist = mild punishment; a fifty = a GBP 50 note; Federali = Mexican federal police
changed his tune = stock phrase; drum = gun magazine / drum kit; click-clack = reloading
Spoons = Wetherspoons pub chain; hobnail boots = heavy studded boots
duck-duck goose = playground game; here a dark twist: a debtor is hit on the head for not paying
Dublin / double it = dealer markup; bubbling carb = cooking crack; lights out / halogen = knocked out / halogen bulb; boxed / packaged = punched / posted; Amazon = next-day delivery
guap = money; red dot = laser sight; dot-dot = double-barrel shotgun; Smackdown = WWE wrestling; wagwan = what's up (London/Jamaican greeting)

legal tender = valid currency; weekender = short-stay tourist; fender bender = minor car crash
upside down = also means owing more than something is worth; 'lose a stone by Sunday' = classic diet-advert promise
bungee operators upsell photo and video packages and write your weight on your hand in marker pen
pavos = Spanish for turkeys and Spanish slang for money
the narrator speaks southern English, non-rhotic (lender / weekender / sender rhyme)
