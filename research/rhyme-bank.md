# Rhyme bank: sound families for the Worldwide track (southern English, Reading/Bristol)

Built 11 Oct 2026 with `toolkit/rhyme_tools.py` (rhymes, mosaic, families; words filtered to common ones a mate would say) plus a pair-checker over the same key functions. All advisory: Joe's ear decides. Accent notes in the last section override the tool where it is US-biased.

Legend for cut-ups (multisyllabic pairs, `A | B`): [p3]/[p2]/[p1] = perfect rhyme on last 3/2/1 syllables, [n2] = near, [a3]/[a2] = vowel pattern only (assonance, a dactylic carpet). "(ear)" = tool could not judge (out-of-dictionary word or accent issue); say it aloud. "(Joe)" = Joe's own seed. "(confirm)" = Joe to confirm.

- gaff / laugh / half / staff / faff / naff: the tool says they rhyme (US). In southern English gaff, faff, naff have the short TRAP vowel; laugh, half, staff, calf, bath have the long BATH vowel. They DO NOT rhyme (gaff ≠ laugh, faff ≠ half). Rhyme gaff with chaff, naff, faff, scaff, Daff.
