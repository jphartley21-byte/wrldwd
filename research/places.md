## Vietnam (Honda Wins, Ha Giang or Hai Van)
- Joe seed: "Rode Honda wins in Vietnam". Honda Winner is a clutch bike, about 350,000 VND/day (about GBP 11) (verified, Ha Giang guide, 2026). Honda Blade (semi-auto) about 180,000 VND; XR150 about 550,000 VND (verified). Fuel about 70,000 VND/day (verified). Insurance is extra. Pre-2020 prices were lower, around 150,000 VND (verified, same source, older). Whether Joe's 2015 "Win" was a Honda Win (the old Honda Wave-like semi-auto step-through) is to confirm.
- Ha Giang Loop: far-north mountain loop, usually 3 days from Hanoi, run by hostels and "easy rider" guides who drive you (verified, Expedia/Mad Monkey sources). Joe's route (Ha Giang or Hai Van) is to confirm. Hai Van Pass, Hue to Da Nang, is not covered by my sources (to confirm).

- Food/drink: pho, banh mi, egg coffee in Hanoi, bia hoi around 10,000-20,000 VND a glass (to confirm), rice wine / "happy water" in mountain homestays (Joe's "rice wine" seed; to confirm local name, often "ruou").

## Laos (Vang Vieng, weed pizza, Luang Prabang)
- Joe seeds: "Weed pizza in laos", "Tubing down a river in laos".
- Vang Vieng tubing on the Nam Song river was a backpacker rite of passage; riverside bars with rope swings and towers; deaths in 2011-12 led to a crackdown (verified, GlobalPost/The World). Reports of 27 tourist deaths in 2011 vary by source (verified as reported, but unclear; avoid quoting a number in a bar). After reopening: few bars, no zip lines or slides (verified, one source).
- Tubing price in older blog: 55,000 kip plus 60,000 kip returnable deposit (about GBP 2 + 2.2) (verified, undated blog; to confirm current).
- "Happy menu": bars offering mushroom shakes, happy pizzas, hash or opium cakes (verified as anecdote only; one blog). Weed is illegal in Laos; frame as joke about menu, not advice.
- Luang Prabang: dawn alms-giving where monks collect rice (verified); tourist misbehaviour is a known problem (verified, tour operators), so keep the bar respectful. Kuang Si Falls about 30 km away, turquoise travertine water, bear rescue centre (verified).
- Words (to confirm): sabaidee = "sa-bye-dee" (hello); khop chai = "kop jai" (thanks); Beerlao = the national lager; kip = currency; "bo pen nyang" = "no worries"; "nyam" = tasty.
- Rhyme words: Laos (ow/now/wow/cow/how, "Lao's" = louse), Nam Song (long/wrong/song), Vang Vieng (ring/thing/sing/king), Luang Prabang (bang/gang/rang), kip (trip/slip/flip/ship), Mekong (song/long), Pak Ou, Phonsavan, Vientiane (vee-en-chan: chance/dance), tube (boob/lube), Beerlao (cow/now).
- Hooks: cows and water buffalo on the road, elephants (once the national symbol, "Land of a Million Elephants"), geckos, bear (Kuang Si), "tubing" = "tube" (London Underground pun), "high pizza" / "pie in the sky".
- Sources: https://theworld.org/stories/2012/02/11/vang-vieng-laos-backpacker-mecca-turned-disaster-magnet ; https://southeastasiabackpacker.com/tubing-vang-vieng-whats-all-the-fuss/ ; https://www.audleytravel.com/us/laos/things-to-do/kuang-si-waterfalls-and-the-surrounding-countryside-full-day ; https://themaritimeexplorer.ca/2023/08/10/alms-giving-in-luang-prabang/

## Thailand (Pai Canyon, Full Moon Party, shroom shakes, Koh Tao scuba)
- Joe seeds: "Pai Canyon", "Full moon party Thailand", "Shrooms shakes Thailand", "Scuba diving".
- Pai Canyon: narrow dusty ridges with no safety rails, donation-based entry, crowded at sunset, don't wear white (verified, travel guides). Pai Walking Street night market sets up about 17:00, peaks 19:00-21:00, closes 22:00-23:00; pad Thai, mango sticky rice, live music (verified). Best season Nov-Feb; cold nights (verified).
- Full Moon Party, Haad Rin beach, Koh Phangan: monthly, around each full moon (to confirm). Entry about 200 THB (about GBP 4.50), bucket 150-250 THB (about GBP 3.50-6), beer 80-150 THB, budget night 1,500-2,200 THB (verified, one consumer guide). Neon body paint, fire skipping ropes, sandcastle-style buckets with Thai whisky/Red Bull (to confirm).
- Shroom shakes: no source found (gap). Joe's seed only. Legal status is murky in Thailand (to confirm); keep it a gag, not an endorsement.
- Koh Tao scuba: Open Water course about 11,000-12,500 THB (about GBP 250-285), common fixed price about 11,000 THB; fun dives 800-1,000 THB each (verified, dive-shop blogs 2026). Jungle Party on Koh Tao about 1,000 THB (verified, one blog).
- Words (to confirm): sawasdee krap/ka = "sa-wat-dee krap/ka"; kop khun krap/ka = thanks; mai pen rai = "my pen rye" (never mind); sanuk = fun; chok dee = cheers; Chang, Singha, Leo = beers; "same same but different" = the traveller T-shirt.
- Sensory: songthaew (shared pickup) rattling, tuk-tuks, 7-Eleven toasties, mosquito coils, fluorescent paint, bass from the beach, jellyfish warnings (to confirm).
- Rhyme words: Pai (eye/high/sky/why/fly/lie), Phangan (bang/gang/rang), Tao (now/wow/how/cow), Koh (go/so/no/slow/flow), Chiang Mai (my/high), Bangkok (rock/block/clock/shock), Chang (bang/gang), baht (hat/that/flat), Khao San (son/sun/fun/done), Phuket (a classic rude rhyme, skip or use knowingly), Krabi (baby/maybe), Samui (phooey/gooey).
